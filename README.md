## **About the Game**  
**One Last Order** is a first-person psychological horror game set in a dimly lit, retro 3D café. You play as a late-night barista just trying to make rent, but as the night progresses, unsettling encounters with strange customers blur the line between reality and paranoia.  

What starts as a mundane shift spirals into a tense, eerie experience—culminating in a chilling revelation.  

### **Genre**  
- Psychological Horror  
- Narrative-Driven  
- Immersive Sim  

### **Art Style**  
- Retro 3D (PSX-inspired)  
- Low-poly models with gritty textures  
- Moody lighting & atmospheric effects  

---  

## **Story & Characters**  
### **The Protagonist**  
A tired café worker struggling to make ends meet. They’re reserved, hardened by life, and just trying to survive another shift—until the night takes a dark turn.  

### **Key Characters**  
- **The Antagonist** – A charming yet unsettling man who seems to know too much about you.  
- **Hobo Joe** – A volatile, erratic stranger who may or may not be a red herring.  
- **Generic Customers** – Mundane NPCs that contrast with the growing tension.  

### **Plot Summary**  
- **Prologue (Apartment):** The protagonist wakes up, prepares for work, and heads out.  
- **Act 1 (Café):** Serve customers, but some interactions grow increasingly disturbing.  
- **Act 2 (Closing Time):** Lock up the café, but something feels… off.  
- **Act 3 (Walking Home):** A tense journey through dark streets, with an unexpected encounter.  
- **Act 4 (The Apartment):** The night’s final twist.
